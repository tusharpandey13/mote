MOTE

Design

Mote, an Android Bluetooth-HID remote for any BT host (Mac first). Revision 2: light (silver aluminium) is the default theme, with dark (titanium) as a variant. One material family: soft-touch caps, recessed wells, dark-anodised primary cap with LED legend, noise texture. Click a board to open it full size; link to one with #<id>. PNG copies live in the repo.
